AI skills

  • Text, image & voice AI
  • Data, examples & model training
  • Patterns, predictions & AI mistakes
  • AI inputs, outputs & responses
  • AI evaluation — accuracy, fairness & trust
  • AI-guided research & idea development

  • Git — version control
  • Website design & publishing
  • Decision rules & computational thinking
  • Testing, debugging & refinement

THINK & COMMUNICATE

Creative & leadership skills

Original ideas get stronger when you can explain them, challenge them and bring others along.

  • Creativity & ideation

  • Evidence-based decisions
  • Communication & collaboration
  • Public speaking & presentation
  • Confidence & project ownership
Age-appropriate depth. Skills that build over time.

Ages 6–8 focus on guided AI prompts, patterns, decision rules and creative expression. Older groups progress to AI features, web coding, Git and deeper evaluation, according to the program. Term classes build depth over several weeks; holiday workshops focus on a three-day project.
