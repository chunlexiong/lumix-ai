Parent FAQs

Find answers about class fit, what students learn and how to plan your visit.

{% icon 'people' %} Choosing a class

How do I find my child’s age group?

Use the age filter on the AI classes page. Age bands can differ between terms and workshops.

Does my child need coding experience?

Younger groups use guided activities and simple rules. Older groups may work with website code and more independent projects. If your child is new to coding, tell us their age and experience so we can help you choose.

How do the January workshop dates work?

The January Future Home Lab runs twice. Choose the three-day session that suits your family. See the dates and age-group times.

What if my child is between two groups?

Share their age, interests and anything they’ve already tried, such as AI prompts or building a website. Ask us which group offers the right level of challenge.

{% icon 'bulb' %} Learning and projects

What is vibe coding?

Students describe a feature to an AI coding assistant, try the code it generates and refine it with teacher guidance. They learn to explain what the code does and check whether it works.

How do students learn to use AI responsibly?

Students practise checking answers, spotting bias and deciding what information to keep private. The depth of these activities varies by age and class.

Will my child practise presenting?

Students practise explaining what they made and why they made it. At Future Home Lab, they present their project at the end of the workshop.

Where can I see student projects?

Student Life is where we share published class photos, LumiX AI videos and student projects.

Where can I check dates, times and fees?

Each listing shows its dates and each age group’s times and fees. Check the AI classes page for current details.

Does my child need to bring a device?

Check with us before the first session so we can confirm the device and setup for your child’s class.

How do I ask about availability?

Email, call or message us on WeChat with the class you’re considering and your child’s age.
